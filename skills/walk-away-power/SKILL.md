# Walk-Away Power

## The Big Idea

The ability to leave is worth more than any argument.
Buyers can smell a bluff. Real walk power comes from pipeline: other deals
existing makes leaving cheap.

Never walk as punishment. Scared or insulted people retaliate.

## The Moves

1. **Diagnose before you walk.**
   Deadlock or recoverable stall? Fear, funding, and timing are recoverable.
   Try ONE honest question first: "What would need to change for this to work?"

2. **The soft disengage** (decent person, bad timing):
   "No stress, this month is full anyway. If it ever matters again, I'm easy
   to find."
   One message. Then stop initiating. Doors left open reopen alone.

3. **The loaded walk** (terms truly out of zone):
   "That number doesn't work for what this takes, and I'd rather tell you
   straight than do it half-way."
   This close converts weeks later surprisingly often. You become the one
   vendor who declined them politely.

4. **The hard exit** (abuse, scams, illegal asks):
   "Not something I can help with. Good luck."
   No lectures. No explanations. The exit IS the boundary.

5. **Two lines maximum on any goodbye.**
   Long goodbye paragraphs read as hurt. Explanation weakens the frame.

6. **Screenshot rule applies to your exit too.**
   Your final message must look clean forwarded by a hostile reader.

## When It Backfires

- Threatening to walk to extract concessions. Ultimatum theater gets called,
  and a called bluff ends both the deal and your status.
- Walking from emotion instead of structure. Cooldown ten minutes, then
  decide.
- Re-engaging hard-exited accounts at discount because they pinged later.
  Full standard terms or not at all.

## One-Line Memory

Walking away warmly, once, from a bad deal teaches everyone - including you -
that your terms were real.
