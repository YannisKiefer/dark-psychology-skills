# Frame Control

## The Big Idea

The person who needs the deal LESS controls the deal.
Needy people get squeezed. Calm people get paid.

You cannot fake this with words. You build it with posture and pipeline.

## The Moves

1. **Never answer twice.**
   Explain once, precisely. If they repeat the question, they are not
   confused, they are negotiating. Answer shorter the second time.

2. **Silence is a move.**
   After you state your price or terms: stop typing.
   The first person to fill the silence usually gives something up.

3. **Slow down when they speed up.**
   They push "today or forget it"? You get calmer and slower.
   Matching panic makes two panicked people.

4. **Statements beat questions.**
   "Your listing has three reviews hurting you." gets replies.
   "Would you maybe be interested in...?" gets deleted.
   Questions beg. Statements invite.

5. **Let small things go.**
   Winning tiny points feels good and loses deals. You are closing, not
   debating.

6. **Walk warm.**
   Leaving politely is stronger than staying angrily:
   "No stress either way. If it ever matters again, I'm easy to find."
   Doors you don't slam reopen by themselves.

## When It Backfires

- Acting unneedied while actually having zero other options. It leaks through
  reply speed and message length. Fix the pipeline first, then the tone.
- Using silence to punish. People feel the difference between calm and sulking.

## One-Line Memory

Power is not taken in the conversation. It is built before it, by not
needing the outcome.
