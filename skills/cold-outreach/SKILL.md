# Cold Outreach

## The Big Idea

Your first message has ONE job: earn the second message.
No price. No pitch. No links. The stranger owes you nothing.

Everyone scans. The first line decides everything.

## The Moves

1. **Prove you looked.**
   Reference something specific about THEM:
   "Noticed your shop has a 1-star sitting right under your phone number."
   Specific attention is a pattern interrupt. Templates are invisible.

2. **Curiosity gap.**
   "There's something specific about your listing that makes this fixable.
   Most owners never hear what."
   An open loop begs one reply to close.

3. **Give value in message one.**
   One true, useful fact costs you nothing and flips the frame from taker
   to giver.

4. **The sequence, not the spam.**
   Touch 1: observation. Touch 2: teach one thing. Touch 3: proof.
   Touch 4: apply it to them + honest scarcity once. Touch 5: short offer.
   Every touch carries NEW value. "Just bumping this" is dead air.

5. **Stop condition.**
   Two silent touches after the pivot means park and return later.
   The relationship outlives any single deal.

6. **Mirror their rhythm.**
   They answer in hours, you wait at least an hour. Never double-text a
   silence on the same day.

7. **Exit with the door open.**
   "Done, won't bother you again. If it ever matters, reply AUDIT and I'll
   check it free." Face-saving exits convert months later.

## When It Backfires

- Links or attachments in touch one. Reads as mass marketing, gets reported.
- Fake urgency anywhere in the arc. One caught lie retroactively poisons all
  five touches.
- Over-personal details (family, health). Reference their business only.

## One-Line Memory

Strangers ignore pitches but remember people who understood their situation
before being asked for anything.
