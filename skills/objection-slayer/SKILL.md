# Objection Slayer

## The Big Idea

An objection is not a no. It is fear wearing a costume.
Answer the fear, and the objection dissolves. Argue with the words, and it
grows.

Rule one: never answer an objection twice. Repetition reads as pressure.

## The Table

| They say | The fear underneath | Your move |
|---|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | Losing money | Shift to cost of NOT acting: "What does this problem cost you monthly?" |
| "Let me think" | Deciding wrong | Give permission + one honest question: "Totally your call. What separates yes from no right now?" Then silence. |
| "Is this a scam?" | Being fooled | Agree first: "Fair guess, this space is full of them." Then invert: proof before payment. |
| "Guarantee it?" | Risking money | Put the guarantee in structure: "You pay only after you see the result." |
| "Other guy is cheaper" | Choosing wrong | Neutral checklist: "Ask him who pays first and for one finished case." Never attack the rival. |
| "I need to ask my partner" | Not looking foolish alone | Arm them: "Send them this summary. Want me to hold your price until Monday?" |
| Silence after quote | All of the above | One new useful fact, then park. Chasing raises fear. |
| "How does it work?" (fishing) | Unknown = danger OR they probe you | Explain the WHAT you get, keep the HOW yours: "The result proves itself today." |

## Universal Laws

1. Validate before you solve. "Fair question" buys more than any argument.
2. One objection, one message, one answer.
3. Objections you hear twice were never answered once.
4. If the same fear shows up three times, name it directly:
   "Something is holding you back. Money, trust, or timing?"
5. Some objections are exits in disguise. Let those go warmly.

## When It Backfires

- Answering objections nobody raised yet, mid-thread. Raise fears FIRST via
  honesty, then stop re-answering settled ones.
- Sarcasm on the third repeat. Calm brevity wins; irritation screenshots badly.

## One-Line Memory

People don't buy against their fears. You either remove the fear or you wait
outside it.
