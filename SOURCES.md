# The Library Behind These Skills

36 books read cover to cover. Military psyop manuals, FBI behavior files,
CIA analysis doctrine, propaganda science, dark psychology, and the classics
of persuasion and direct response.

Everything in `skills/` is distilled from these - in original words.
No book text is reproduced anywhere in this repo.

## The Honest Line

Several books on that shelf teach manipulation directly.
Every tactic here passed one filter: **does it still work after being fully
explained to the other person?**

Manipulation only works while hidden. That is exactly why it fails long-term -
and why this pack teaches seeing it faster than doing it.
