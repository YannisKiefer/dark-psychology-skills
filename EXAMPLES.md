# Example Threads

Two short examples of the skills in action. Generic business: a web designer
selling a site fix to a skeptical restaurant owner over DM.

## Example 1: The Skeptic

**Buyer:** is this another scam? had 2 "experts" already, both vanished

**You:** Fair guess - this space is full of people who charge first and
disappear. That's why I do it backwards: you pay nothing until the new site
is live and you've clicked through it yourself.

> Skills used: accusation audit (discovery-power), risk reversal
> (risk-reversal)

**Buyer:** and if I hate the design?

**You:** Then you've seen it, hated it, and paid zero. You lose an hour of
looking. I lose the work. All the risk sits on my side on purpose.

> Skills used: burden shift (trust-from-zero)

**Buyer:** ok send me what you'd change

## Example 2: The Price Grinder

**Buyer:** $2000?? guy on fiverr does it for 300

**You:** Worth asking him one question: who pays first? Mine runs the other
way - live site first, money after.

> Skills used: neutral competitor check (objection-slayer), structure over
> price defense

**Buyer:** still, 1500 and we start

**You:** Below what this takes doesn't work for me, and I'd rather tell you
straight than go half-way.

> Skills used: false-zone rejection (price-anchor), loaded walk
> (walk-away-power)

**Buyer:** wait - what if I bring you 2 more restaurants too?

**You:** Three sites together drops it to $1,600 each, since the setup work
overlaps. That's the plan rate - you're qualifying for it, not squeezing it.

> Skills used: earned tier (price-anchor), trade not gift

**Buyer:** deal. sending the other owners your way tonight

## What to Notice

- Nobody argued about price. The frame moved from "is he expensive" to
  "who carries the risk" and "what qualifies me for the better rate".
- Every message stays under 4 lines.
- Every claim could survive being forwarded.
